🧭 Recommended Strategy

⚖️
Sept 30 for the full target is not reachable. It needs about 9,775 validations a day, well above the best day so far (2,315). The best case reaches about 40% by Sept 30. Recommend the Central Office a realistic Sept 30 milestone of about 40%, and full completion by Oct 15.
🎯
Oct 15 is within reach with weekends plus the step-up. On weekdays it needs about 2,607/day. Working 7 days a week lowers the need to about 1,862/day, and the gap plan with weekends finishes around Oct 14.
👥
Capacity levers: reactivate the 15 validators idle on Sept 25 (+280/day at median pace) and lift the active validators below the median of 45 (+212/day). Together daily capacity moves from 1,665 toward about 2,156–2,262.
🗺️
Redeploy toward the biggest balances. The 10 largest LGU balances hold about 35% of the remaining target; the 33 not-yet-started LGUs hold a large share, including City of Talisay (1,277), City of Mandaue (953 remaining) and Tubigon (976). Assign dedicated validator teams to those first.
🚨
Daily control: give each LGU a daily quota (balance ÷ working days) and review every morning. Sept 26 output dropped 74.8% from Sept 25 — daily follow-up is essential. Encode the same day: past days keep being revised upward, which hides the true pace.
